Project Full Title: Enceladus Simulation


Short Name: Enceladus


Completed Date: 07/30/26


Medium: Digital, Projection


Course Info: MAT 111FS, Nefeli Manoudaki


Abstract: 

A projection of the lunar surface of Saturn’s moon, Enceladus. It is an immersive experience that allows a viewer to interact with it by controlling movement across a preset path across the lunar surface. The movement is controlled via hand movement across a sensor. The projection surrounds the viewer along 2 walls, giving it a grand scale. There were additional 3D printed ice structures that provided decor around the hand sensor. 

We used a live projection via TouchDesigner. Motion was detected by a Leap Motion Controller by putting one’s hands above the sensor and moving it left/right. Preset locations were set through TouchDesigner’s camera viewport, and the sequence of presets were triggered when movement was detected. The landscape itself was created in Blender.
