About

I'm an engineering student at UC Santa Barbara interested in the places where hardware, software, and physical materials meet — sensors that read a room, small circuits with a single job, structures built to hold up a projection. Most of what's here started as a class project and turned into something I kept tinkering with after the deadline passed.

Outside of coursework, I like building things with my hands as much as writing code for them — 3D printing, breadboarding, and the occasional turtle-related engineering problem.
