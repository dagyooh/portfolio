enceladus

digital, projection | 2026 | group | mat 111fs — nefeli manoudaki

a projection of the lunar surface of saturn's moon, enceladus, that a viewer explores by moving their hand over a sensor — surrounded by 3d-printed ice structures.

enceladus is an immersive experience that allows a viewer to interact with it by controlling movement across a preset path across the lunar surface. the movement is controlled via hand movement across a sensor. the projection surrounds the viewer along two walls, giving it a grand scale. additional 3d-printed ice structures provided decor around the hand sensor.

we used a live projection via touchdesigner. motion was detected by a leap motion controller by putting one's hands above the sensor and moving left or right. preset locations were set through touchdesigner's camera viewport, and the sequence of presets triggered when movement was detected. the landscape itself was created in blender.

this was a group project done with four other people: bruno klena, rose li, taj draper, and ajoonee sidhu. i 3d printed the ice structures, and worked on integrating the sensor data to the landscape in touchdesigner with rose li.

turtle timer

physical hardware | 2025 | independent | me 10 — geoff tsai

a timer system that automatically shuts off my turtles' heat lamp after 3, 4, or 6 hours, so no one in my family has to remember to do it.

i decided to build a timer since turning off the lamp had always been a hassle for my family members — it was the most frequent chore in taking care of the turtles. the project has four pushbuttons with time settings of 3, 4, and 6 hours, and a reset button.

when the timer finishes, a servo motor activates and turns off the lamp switch. the timer was built with an arduino nano, pushbuttons, wires, legos, and a custom 3d-printed part.
